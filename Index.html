Practicing Islam in Non-Majority Environments: Mauritius

دراسة حالة: المسلمون في موريشيوس

This dedicated empirical research module examines how a minority Muslim population (~17.3%–18.2% of Mauritius) preserves religious traditions while navigating complex legal frameworks (Waqf Act 1941, FCC Act 2023), statutory reporting deadlines, and secular democratic state regulations.

Waqf Act 1941 • Legal Duties Mauritius

Mosque Management & Mutawalli Obligations

Under the Waqf Act 1941, mosque managers (Mutawallis) must balance spiritual leadership with strict legal mandates, ensuring property maintenance takes priority ("First Charge" Rule) and capping administrative pay at 10%.

April 1st Filing • AML/CFT Timeline

Statutory Financial Deadlines & AML Audits

Details the strict annual timeline: Jan 1st logbook opening, April 1st audited accounts deadline with the Waqf Board, and continuous Anti-Money Laundering compliance required under the Financial Crimes Commission (FCC) Act 2023.
